# Privacy Policy — Construction Calculator by NEXERA-IN

**Last updated:** 2026/08/30.

NEXERA-IN ("we", "us", "our") operates the Construction Calculator mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

## Summary

Construction Calculator is designed to work entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or processes your data. We do not have user accounts. We do not show ads. We do not use analytics or tracking software.

## Information the App Accesses

**Camera and Photo Library**
The App requests camera and photo library access only when you choose to photograph or upload a roof drawing for the Roofing Calculator's measurement feature. Images you capture or select are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded to us or any third party. If you use the App's Share feature to send a generated PDF, the image itself is not included — only the calculated results are.

**Calculation Data and History**
Everything you type into the App — measurements, item names, calculation results, and your History of past calculations — is stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to us. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

**Web Search**
Some calculators include an optional "Search the web" shortcut. If you use it, your typed search query is sent to your device's default web browser to perform a standard web search (e.g. via Google) — the same as if you had typed it into your browser yourself. We do not see, log, or store these queries.

**Sharing and Export**
If you choose to Share or export a calculation as a PDF, that file is generated on your device and handed to your device's own share menu, where you choose the destination (email, WhatsApp, printing, saving to files, etc.). We are not involved in and do not see this transfer.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect: your name, email address, location, contacts, or any personally identifiable information. We do not use cookies, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device.

## Children's Privacy

The App is a professional trade tool intended for construction industry use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. Standard operating system features (your device's camera, file picker, share sheet, and web browser) are used as described above, and are governed by your device manufacturer's and browser's own privacy practices, not ours.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
