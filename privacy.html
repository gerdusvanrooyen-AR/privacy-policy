Privacy Policy — Construction Calculator

Effective date: 1 September 2026 · Last updated: 1 September 2026

Construction Calculator (“the app”) is published by NEXERA-IN. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version. The app does not have accounts, does not require you to sign in, and does not send your calculations anywhere. Everything you type stays on your own phone unless you choose to send it.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves the following on your phone only, so your work is still there when you reopen it:

  • the figures you type into the calculators, and the results worked out from them
  • your calculation history, including the material list each calculation produced
  • your settings — language, measurement system, and similar preferences
  • the material quantities you enter as available yard stock
  • the name and contact details you type into a problem report, if you type any, so that you do not have to type them again next time

This data is held in your phone’s own app storage. It is not uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Clearing the app’s data from your phone’s settings deletes it. Inside the app, History has its own Clear, and the yard stock sheet has one too.

Camera, photos and files

Some calculators let you attach a picture or a file — a photo you take, an image already on your phone, or a PDF — as a visual reference while you type. The roof outline screen is the only place this happens.

The app asks your permission before using the camera or your photo library.

No measurement is taken from the picture. Every figure comes from the tape readings you type; a drawing has no scale the phone can read. The picture is there so you can see which side is which while you work.

Pictures and files are handled entirely on your device. When you choose an existing file, your phone copies it into the app’s own temporary storage so it can be displayed, and a PDF’s first page is converted to an image there as well. Those copies live in the app’s cache on your phone and go when the app’s data does. Nothing is uploaded to us and nothing is sent to any server.

When information leaves your phone — only when you send it

There are four places where you, not the app, can send information out. Each one needs a deliberate tap, and in each one your phone hands the information to another app that you choose.

  1. Sharing a result. When you share or export a calculation as a PDF, your phone’s own share menu opens and you choose where it goes — email, WhatsApp, a printer, or a file. The app builds the document on the phone and hands it to your phone’s share menu; it does not transmit it itself, and it does not keep a copy.
  2. Reporting a problem. If you use the report button, the app opens your own email app with a message already written, addressed to NEXERA-IN. That message includes what you typed in the report, the app version, your device platform, which calculator was open, your measurement system, your language, and the figures on screen at the time — this is what makes a fault possible to fix. The app shows you all of it on screen before you send. Nothing is sent until you press send in your own email app, and you can read and edit the message first.
  3. Suggesting a calculator. The same, through your phone’s share menu rather than your email app: you pick where the message goes and you send it yourself.
    Reports and suggestions you send are received at gerdusvanrooyen@gmail.com and are used only to fix faults and decide what to build next.
  4. Searching the web. Where a calculator offers a web search, tapping it opens a Google search in your browser with the words you chose. From that point Google’s own privacy policy applies, not this one. Nothing the browser returns comes back into a calculation.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect analytics, usage tracking, or advertising identifiers.
  • It does not show adverts.
  • It does not sell or share personal information.
  • It does not use your location.
  • It does not require your name, address, phone number or identity number. The problem report form offers a name and contact field so we can reply to you; both are optional and both are used only for that.
  • It makes no network request of its own. There is no code in the app that contacts a server.

Permissions the app declares

  • Camera — real, and used in one place: photographing a roof drawing, as described above.
  • Internet — declared by the framework the app is built with, not used by the app. The app has no code that opens a network connection.
  • Storage (Android 12 and older) and Vibrate — declared by the components the app is built from, and not used by the app’s own code.

Children

The app is a work tool for the building trade and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Because the data is on your own device, you control it: you can clear your history in the app, clear your yard stock in the app, clear the app’s data in your phone’s settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of it. If you have emailed us a report and want it deleted, write to gerdusvanrooyen@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

This policy is written to meet South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and the equivalent expectations of the Google Play and Apple App Store listings.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts doing something this policy does not describe, the policy is updated before that version is published, and the date at the top changes.
